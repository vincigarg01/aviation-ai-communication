✈️ Airline AI Service Desk
A verification-led workflow for flight disruption communications
Clear updates. Checked facts. Consistent messages.
A fictional airline service project exploring how prompt design and human review can help teams communicate changing flight information.

The challenge
During a disruption, passengers need accurate updates across emails, announcements, SMS and service desk responses. Incorrect timings, unsupported benefit claims or inconsistent messages can cause confusion.
This project develops a structured workflow for drafting, verifying and updating passenger communications using fictional flight scenarios.
What the project includes
📣 Passenger messages	🔎 Source verification	🌐 Consistency checks
Emails, announcements, SMS and service desk responses	Checks for factual errors, missing details and unsupported claims	Updated timings and English–Hindi message review


🧩 Prompting strategies	🔄 Operational updates	📝 Reusable template
Comparison of six prompting strategies	Revised timings applied across communications	Email prompt tested on a separate flight scenario


Workflow
01 · Establish the facts
Record the supplied flight details, policy and information gaps.
02 · Draft with structured prompts
Create messages suited to the task and passenger audience.
03 · Check the content
Evaluate accuracy, completeness, clarity and tone against the source.
04 · Correct and adapt
Remove unsupported claims and tailor messages for each channel.
05 · Apply operational updates
Replace outdated timings across all passenger communications.
06 · Verify and reuse
Check English–Hindi consistency and test the reusable prompt on another flight.
Verification scenarios
Scenario	What to verify
SB204 · Initial update	Scheduled departure 2:00 PM; expected departure 4:30 PM. Calculate the 2 hour 30 minute delay and check voucher eligibility under the fictional policy.
SB204 · Revised update	Change the expected departure to 5:15 PM and the next passenger update to 4:15 PM.
Missing details	Treat gate, refund and hotel information as unconfirmed; do not invent answers.
Departure wording	Preserve the difference between an expected departure and a guaranteed departure.
SB310 · Template test	Scheduled departure 10:00 AM; expected departure 11:15 AM. Confirm the 1 hour 15 minute delay is below the fictional two-hour voucher threshold.


Deliverables

- Passenger communication pack, reviewed against the source
- Evaluation and correction tables
- English–Hindi email pair
- Reusable email prompt, tested on SB310

Responsible AI use
AI assists with drafting, evaluation and translation. The contributor checks outputs against the supplied facts, records corrections and reviews the final communications.
Scenario boundary: Flight details and policies are fictional. The project is not connected to live airline systems, and no messages are sent to actual passengers.
